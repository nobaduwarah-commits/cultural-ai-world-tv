# Cultural AI World TV

Cultural AI World TV is an AI-powered TV experience designed to help users discover and explore cultures from around the world through an interactive, TV-friendly interface.

## 🌍 What is Cultural AI World TV?

Cultural AI World TV brings cultural discovery to the TV screen. Users can explore countries and regions and discover festivals, traditional dance, food, music, history, and local cultural stories through video and AI-assisted exploration.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🌍 Explore cultures from different countries and regions
- 🤖 AI-powered cultural discovery
- 🔎 Search for countries and cultural topics
- 🎬 Cultural videos and stories
- 🎤 Voice and search-based discovery
- 📺 TV-friendly large-screen interface
- 🎮 Remote / D-pad navigation
- 🇮🇳 Indian regional culture exploration
- ❤️ Favorites and easy navigation
- 🧭 AI Journey for cultural discovery

## 📺 TV Experience

The application is designed specifically for a large-screen TV experience. The interface focuses on clear navigation, large visual elements, remote/D-pad interaction, and simple content discovery.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Open Cultural AI World TV.
2. Explore the available cultural sections.
3. Browse countries and regions.
4. Use search or AI Journey to discover cultural topics.
5. Select cultural videos and explore the content.
6. Navigate the application using the TV remote / D-pad.

## 💡 Inspiration

The project was inspired by the idea of making cultural learning more engaging and accessible through television.

Instead of using TV only for entertainment, Cultural AI World TV aims to turn the TV into an interactive window to the world's cultures.

## 🧩 Challenges

Some of the main challenges included implementing smooth TV remote navigation, handling video playback, designing a TV-friendly interface, and testing the application in an emulator environment.

Working with TV-specific navigation also required a different approach compared with mobile or web applications because users interact primarily through a remote control.

## 📚 What I Learned

Through this project, I learned more about TV-focused application development, large-screen UI/UX design, remote/D-pad navigation, video playback, emulator testing, and AI-assisted cultural discovery.
